Unit 3 exercise draft: nutrition-label OCR -> Atwater factors (work in progress)

Notebook: activities/u03_nutrition_ocr.edit.py (not yet linked from any page)

Pipeline (all in the browser, no server):
  1. mo.ui.file upload (several photos) or four embedded sample labels
     (potato chips, cocoa powder, beef jerky, bamboo shoots; grayscale JPEG, 900 px, q55).
  2. Per-photo crop sliders (percent of width/height) with a red-box preview.
  3. Lighting correction in Python: half-resolution grey closing -> background,
     divide, rescale long side to 1800 px, unsharp mask. Sent as JPEG data URLs.
  4. Tesseract.js 5.1.1 (WASM) inside an anywidget, loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net
     (tesseract.js, tesseract.js-core, @tesseract.js-data/eng@1.0.0 best_int, ~3 MB).
     Each label is rotated -2, 0, +2 degrees on a canvas and read in page modes 6 and 11
     (two workers in parallel): six readings per label.
  5. Regex parser (English/French names; "g" misread as 8/9/q; "Og" -> 0 g; "05g" -> 0.5)
     and a majority vote per nutrient, with an agreement table.
  6. Editable CSV text area (human check), multiselect of three foods,
     np.linalg.solve for (a, b, c) and cond(A).

Tested locally (marimo run + headless Chromium, CDN files served from npm copies):
  OCR result for the four samples:
    potato chips  490, 30, 51.6, 3.6
    cocoa powder   20, 0.5, 3, 1
    beef jerky     60, 1, 5, 9
    bamboo shoots  15, 0.5, 2, 0
  All 16 values correct with the 900 px samples. With 1000 px samples the cocoa
  carbohydrate (curved can) was not found and showed "?" until edited by hand,
  so expect occasional misses on student photos; the editable table handles that.
  A full Pyodide run could not be tested here (CDN blocked in this container);
  `marimo export html-wasm` succeeds (about 1.45 MB HTML).

Science results with the true label values (kcal/g for fat, carbohydrate, protein):
  chips, cocoa, jerky     ->  9.00, 4.02, 3.43   cond 67
  chips, cocoa, bamboo    ->  6.22, 5.94, -0.94  cond 141
  chips, jerky, bamboo    ->  5.47, 6.13, 2.65   cond 103
  cocoa, jerky, bamboo    -> 22.5,  1.88, 3.12   cond 78
  least squares (all 4)   ->  7.95, 4.65, 3.18
  The spread comes from label rounding (calories under 50 kcal are rounded to 5 kcal,
  grams to 0.5 or 1 g) amplified by the condition number, and from fibre counted at
  ~2 kcal/g inside carbohydrate. This fits L12 (conditioning, perturbed right-hand side).
