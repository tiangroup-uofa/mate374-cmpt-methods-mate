Numerical setup
---------------
Lennard-Jones reduced units; 13 equal masses, no cutoff or external bath.
Fixed random displacement direction, seed 374.
Displacement amplitude chosen so U(0) = U_min + N * added_energy.
All initial velocities zero.
Force derivative spacing delta = 1e-5, distinct from time step.
Verlet velocity is recovered at CURRENT time n, using n-1 and n+1.
Previous position initialized from x0 - dt*v0 + 0.5*dt²*F0.
Energy recorded at every step, not just animation frames.
Fixed view; atoms outside the view are counted and clipped explicitly.

Restructure (Oct 28, instructor request)
----------------------------------------
Order: what we can compute (pair energy V = energy_func, internal energy U,
FD forces) -> minimization check -> forward Euler -> Stormer-Verlet ->
step functions -> assemble engine -> energy diagnostics and caveats.
Removed the three-frames figure and its motion table.
Terminology: U = internal energy; V = pairwise energy (energy_func in code);
forces(pos) = central differences of internal_energy over all 3N
coordinates (no dVdr, no pair-derivative force); step_euler(pos, vel, F, dt)
and step_verlet(pos, pos_prev, F, dt), both with m=1.0.
Notebooks rewritten to these names. Starter TODOs, in lecture order:
the F[i, k] central difference, step_euler, step_verlet. The minimization
check appears once forces is finite; each run waits for the selected step.
The completed notebook opens on forward Euler; time step 0.001 was dropped
from its menu because FD forces make a 10000-step run slow in the browser
(about 1.5 ms per force call natively; dt=0.005 run about 3 s natively).

Minimization check (BFGS, jac = -forces, noisy icosahedral guess, seed 21):
guess U = -37.41088; relaxed U = -44.326801419534 in 51 iterations;
max |F| at minimum 4.8e-8; max |sum F| 7e-10.
With the wrong sign (jac = +forces) BFGS stops at the guess, 0 iterations.

Measured results with FD forces (time 10, added energy 0.3 per atom):
Verlet max |E-E0|: dt=.005 0.0095859, dt=.002 0.0015334, dt=.001 0.00038345
Euler dt=.005: energy gain 49.6556; largest final radius 12.2 (Verlet 1.13).
Verlet dt=.08: overflow stop at t=1.04.
Added energy 1, Verlet dt=.005: max error 0.0623; remains bound.

Interpretive limits
-------------------
Energy conservation alone does not prove trajectory accuracy.
The higher-energy run shows motion; melting needs structural/time statistics.
The class compares forward Euler and position Verlet only. Existing
implicit-Simpson research is outside this lecture's scope.
