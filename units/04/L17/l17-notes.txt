Corrections to the board
------------------------
- f''(x) central formula: the denominator is h^2. The board has 2h.
- f(x-2h) expansion: the cubic term is -(2h)^3/3! f'''. The board shows +.
- 3-point forward: the board's result, f' - (h^2/3) f''', is correct. The page
  writes it with the numerator -3f(x) + 4f(x+h) - f(x+2h) over 2h. That is
  the same formula, with the sign moved from the denominator.
