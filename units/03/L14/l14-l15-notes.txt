Shared example
--------------
- The middle atom between two fixed atoms (a = 1, k = 1, l0 = 1.2a) is the L14 minimizer
  landscape and the L15 saddle example. From (0.3, 0.1), pure Newton converges to the
  saddle at the origin in 4 steps (from (1.5, 0.05) it converges to (l0, 0)); GD, NM, CG,
  BFGS and L-BFGS-B reach the minimum (0, 0.6633). This motivates L15 from L14.
